Child safety standards

Date is a dating app for adults. Child sexual abuse and exploitation has no place on it, in any form, and we act on it rather than wait to be asked. This page is our published standard, and it is the one we hold ourselves to.

Date is 18 and over

Everyone gives their date of birth when they create an account. Anyone under 18 is stopped at that screen and cannot go further. The app is rated 18+ on the store, and every profile on it belongs to an adult.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete everything on it. We do not need a report to do this and we do not ask the account holder for permission.

What is never allowed

These are removal offences. There is no warning, no strike system and no second account:

  • Child sexual abuse material. Any sexual imagery of a person under 18 — photographed, drawn, edited or generated.
  • Sexualising a minor in a profile, a message, a voice note, a photo or a video, including in text alone.
  • Grooming. Contacting, or trying to contact, someone under 18 for a sexual purpose, or building a relationship with them towards one.
  • Sextortion — threatening to share someone's intimate images, and soliciting intimate images from a minor.
  • Trafficking of a minor, or arranging their sexual exploitation.
  • Advertising, linking to, trading or asking for any of the above, on Date or anywhere else.

How to report it, inside the app

Every profile and every conversation carries a report button. It takes two taps and the person is never told who reported them.

  • From a profile: open it, scroll to the bottom, tap Report, then pick a reason.
  • From a conversation: open the chat menu and tap Report.
  • Block sits next to it. Blocking hides you from each other everywhere, immediately, and they are not told.

If it is urgent, or it concerns a child, you can also write to us directly at hello@meetondate.app. You do not need an account to send that email.

What we do when a report arrives

Reports about child safety go to the top of the queue. A person reads them — they are not filed into something automated that nobody opens.

  • We remove the content and close the account.
  • We preserve the account, the messages and the images involved rather than deleting them, so there is something for the authorities to act on.
  • We keep the record of the report even if the reported account is deleted. If closing an account erased its safety record, anyone could clear one by leaving and signing up again.

Reporting to the authorities

Where we find apparent child sexual abuse material, or conduct that appears to sexually exploit a minor, we report it to the competent authorities. For us that means the Polícia Judiciária in Portugal, the relevant national authority in the country concerned, and the CyberTipline of the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where it applies.

We comply with the child safety laws that apply in the countries where Date is available, and we respond to lawful requests from law enforcement.

Who to contact

Our designated contact for child safety — for users, for Google, and for law enforcement — is hello@meetondate.app. Write to that address about our practices, our compliance, or a specific case, and a person will answer it.

Where this sits

These standards sit alongside our terms and our privacy policy, and they meet Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy for social and dating apps. Where anything here is stricter than those documents, this page is what we follow.
