What Date is

Date helps people plan a real date instead of endless chatting. Every profile shows the date its owner actually wants. When two people like each other, they pick a plan and meet. Use it for that: meeting people genuinely and respectfully.

Who can use Date

You must be at least 18 years old. One account per person, made for yourself, with your real name and recent photos of you. No accounts for businesses, promotions or anyone else. If we find an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

Your content

Your photos, bio and messages stay yours. By posting them you let us store them, resize and re-encode them so they load quickly and carry no hidden location data, and show them inside the app to the people the app is designed to show them to. That is the whole point of a profile. This permission covers nothing else: we never sell your content, never use it in advertising, and never show it outside the app, and it ends when you delete the content or your account. Posting also tells us the content is yours to post (your own photos, your own words) and that it breaks no law and takes nothing from anyone else. If that turns out to be untrue and someone brings a claim against us because of it, the cost of that claim is yours rather than ours.

How to behave

Be the person someone would want a second date with. No harassment, hate, threats, nudity without consent, spam, scams or asking people for money. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users: reported content is reviewed, normally within 24 hours, and accounts that break these rules can be warned, suspended or removed without notice. You can report and block anyone. Blocking makes you invisible to each other and the other person is never told. If we remove something you posted or limit your account, we tell you what it was and why, and you can reply to that message to argue with it: a person reads the reply and can put it back.

Dates happen in real life

Date suggests meeting in public places and the app includes a safety checklist before every confirmed date: public spot, your own transport there and back, and a friend who knows where you are. Use it. You alone decide whether and where to meet someone; we cannot vouch for any person on the app, verified badge or not, and we are not part of what happens when you meet.

What you send in chat

Photos, videos and GIFs only reach someone who has turned them on for you, and either of you can turn that off at any time. Text and voice notes always work. Do not share private conversations, photos or recordings from a chat without the other person’s consent.

Deleting takes your conversations with it, and a conversation has two sides, so it disappears from the other person’s app as well, so it is worth saying goodbye first.
