What Date is

Date helps people plan a real first date instead of endless chatting. Every profile shows the date its owner actually wants. When two people like each other, they pick a plan and meet. Use it for that — meeting people genuinely and respectfully.

Who can use Date

You must be at least 18 years old. One account per person, made for yourself, with your real name and recent photos of you. No accounts for businesses, promotions or anyone else.

Your content

Your photos, bio and messages stay yours. By posting them you let us show them inside the app to the people the app is designed to show them to — that is the whole point of a profile. We never sell your content or use it in advertising.

How to behave

Be the person someone would want a second date with. No harassment, hate, threats, nudity without consent, spam, scams or asking people for money. You can report and block anyone — blocking makes you invisible to each other and the other person is never told. We can warn, suspend or remove accounts that break these rules.

Dates happen in real life

Date suggests meeting in public places and the app includes a safety checklist before every confirmed date: public spot, your own transport there and back, and a friend who knows where you are. Use it. You alone decide whether and where to meet someone; we cannot vouch for any person on the app, verified badge or not.

Calls and messages

Voice and video calls only work when the other person has turned on "Accept calls" for you, and either of you can turn that off at any time. Do not record calls or share private conversations without the other person’s consent.

Premium features

Date is free today. Paid extras may arrive later (for example, seeing who liked you). They will be optional, priced clearly, and billed through the app stores with their standard cancellation rules.

Leaving and losing access

You can sign out anytime, and permanently delete your account and everything attached to it from your Profile tab. We can suspend or close accounts that break these terms or the law, and remove content that does.

Honesty about the service

Date is provided as it is today. Features can change, improve or be removed as the app grows. We work to keep it available and safe, but no app can promise zero interruptions or that every profile is genuine.

Changes and contact

If these terms change in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change applies. Questions or requests: hello@meetondate.app.

O que é o Date

Quem pode usar o Date

Tens de ter pelo menos 18 anos. Uma conta por pessoa, feita para ti, com o teu nome real e fotos recentes tuas. Nada de contas para negócios, promoções ou outras pessoas.

O Date sugere encontros em sítios públicos e a app inclui uma checklist de segurança antes de cada encontro confirmado: sítio público, transporte próprio de ida e volta, e uma amiga que sabe onde estás. Chamadas e mensagens

As chamadas de voz e vídeo só funcionam quando a outra pessoa ativou "Aceitar chamadas" para ti, e qualquer um pode desligar isso a qualquer momento. Não graves chamadas nem partilhes conversas privadas sem o consentimento da outra pessoa.

Hoje o Date é gratuito. Extras pagos podem chegar mais tarde (por exemplo, ver quem gostou de ti). Serão opcionais, com preços claros e cobrados através das lojas de apps com as regras normais de cancelamento.
